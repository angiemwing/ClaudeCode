School eligibility · IEP
To qualify for an IEP, your child must meet both:
1
Meet criteria for 1 of 14 disability categories
Autism is one of 14 categories under IDEA — the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act
2
Require specialized services to access education
Even with a medical diagnosis, if the school determines your child can learn without special services, they may not qualify
If both criteria are met
Your child receives an IEP — individualized goals, specialized instruction, and services at no cost

Bottom line
You may need to pursue both systems separately
Step 1
Get a medical evaluation
Unlocks insurance-covered therapies: ABA, speech, and occupational therapy. Avela evaluations available in days, not months.
Step 2
Request a school evaluation
A medical diagnosis does not automatically trigger school services. Request a separate evaluation from your school district.
Step 3
IEP or 504 Plan
Free accommodations and services through your child's school once eligibility is confirmed.
Starting early means your child gets support faster — fewer gaps, fewer delays.
